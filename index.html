About Nomli

Nomli is a social app for everyday life nearby.

Post what you’re up to, follow people you actually know, message and call them, and browse a local Market for food, fashion, and services — all in one place.

Built for real circles, not infinite strangers. Rated 16+. Made by Nomli Tech in Finland.

  • Discover & Friends A feed for posts you care about, plus people worth following.
  • Chat & audio calls Keep the conversation going — voice when text isn’t enough.
  • Local Market Listings next to the same chats — trade nearby without another app.
